Frequently Asked Questions

1. How can I submit a complaint?

Login to your account and select Submit Complaint.

2. How can I track my complaint?

Open Track Complaint and enter your Complaint ID.

3. How can I check complaint status?

Visit My Complaints to view your complaint status.

4. Can I submit multiple complaints?

Yes, students can submit multiple complaints.

5. How can I contact the college?

Visit the Contact Us page for contact information.
